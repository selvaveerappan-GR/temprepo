1. Go to https://developer.microsoft.com/graph/graph-explorer.
2. Click Sign in (top right) and sign in with your account for the POC tenant, where policy 939b9d4e-… lives. If you're already signed in to another tenant, sign out first.
3. Set the method to GET and the version to beta, then paste the URL for the first id:
https://graph.microsoft.com/beta/deviceManagement/groupPolicyConfigurations('939b9d4e-abb8-4fe2-9e45-a73778c84aae')/definitionValues('52c52d07-171d-46b1-b97a-f3da66a65fa7')/presentationValues?$expand=presentation
4. Click Run query. If it answers 403, open the Modify permissions tab and consent to DeviceManagementConfiguration.Read.All. If your organisation requires admin consent and you can't grant it, use option 2 or 3.
5. Copy the response, swap in the next id, and repeat for all six.

Option 2: PowerShell, all six in one go

If the Microsoft Graph PowerShell module is installed (Install-Module Microsoft.Graph.Authentication -Scope CurrentUser if not):

Connect-MgGraph -Scopes DeviceManagementConfiguration.Read.All -TenantId <poc-tenant-id-or-domain>
